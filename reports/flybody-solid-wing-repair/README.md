# Native contact and wing-frame repair

**The autonomous behavioral target still fails.** The current original-UI run advances one actual BANC brain and native FlyBody for 2.010 simulated seconds, with unchanged source hashes and no runtime errors. It first overturns at 0.144 s, ingests no food, and completes no qualified flight or landing. Its lone recorded localization event is explicitly only a bearing-orientation proxy. The 20 screenshots and 2,010 body samples are a new short observation, separate from the earlier 200-image, one-hour audit.

Implemented changes:

- The bowl alone uses a heightfield. Separate static fruit solids follow the original rendered banana and apple meshes, preserving space underneath them. Native support, initial foot placement and food taste recognize the actual contacted geometry. Bowl contact beneath fruit does not become taste.
- The wing lookup now balances aerodynamic moments about the whole-body center of mass. The former fit balanced about the thorax origin. Native masses, inertia, servo, 235.8 Hz frequency and prior mean-force curve are retained; no root force or navigation controller was added.
- Seven verified sugar inputs are restored, a source-conflicted identity is excluded, and 360 explicitly auditory-frequency neurons no longer receive unsupported static-posture drive. Frozen antennae supply native zero motion rather than the old display oscillator.

The causal replay matches the captured native trajectory exactly up to the first wing impact at 0.13805 s. At that point the fly is already tilted about 66 degrees. Removing only wing–environment contact eligibility in the diagnostic prevents the subsequent full inversion and explosive upward launch for the captured 0.4 s, but retains substantial loss of balance. All other contact pairs remain unchanged. Contact deletion is **not** a production change. Arbitrary 5/10 ms wing-contact time constants also fail and were rejected.

The [claw-control replay](../flybody-claw-release/result.json) identifies another contributor before impact. With the same captured neural rates and muscle dynamics, zeroing only hind-claw adhesion delays 60-degree tilt from 0.12265 to 0.22435 s and inversion from 0.14375 to 0.30435 s. It still overturns. Zeroing all six claw controls also fails. These interventions preserve geometry, other control equations and zero external root forces; they demonstrate that simultaneous wing drive and rear-foot grip aggravate the loss of balance. No automatic claw release is installed in production.

The remaining work has distinct physical and neural parts. The separate [passive wing-flex prototype](../flybody-wing-flex-prototype/README.md), constrained by [measured local stiffness of living Drosophila wings](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6361194/), passed small-load force tests but **failed dynamic replay**: inversion at 0.1242 s preceded its first impact at 0.15205 s, and bending reached about 172 degrees, far outside calibration. A rigid split control reproduced the original pre-impact trajectory after an unintended added-fluid-force bug was corrected. The flexible model is rejected for production; passing a static stiffness test does not establish flight efficacy.

The neural model still lacks calibrated signed, phase-sensitive haltere feedback. Normal tarsal stimulation does not recruit the tested probing motor channels, and a 625-configuration mouth-only reach grid cannot touch fruit while the initial body/leg posture is held. The [interface audit](../banc-interface-audit/README.md) also proves that the configured chemical neuromodulator gain multiplies zero current for every gain-bearing cell, and documents the generic 80 Hz full-muscle-excitation scale. In the current live trace, flight-motor rates exceed that scale by 48 ms while the tested probing channels remain silent. These findings do not justify replacing the BANC controller or claiming the full food-seeking cycle works.

The subsequent [21-condition neural gain assay](../banc-interface-audit/gain-assay.json) reproduces four existing reference conditions exactly, including cumulative motor and total network spikes. Each diagnostic uses 200 ms of held input followed by 100 ms without added input. At the current gain, tarsal taste at 25, 75 and 150 Hz with held initial body feedback never recruits m9/m4b, while DLM output changes nonmonotonically. Half the chemical gain removes DLM output but leaves tarsal probing absent and weakens the labellar positive control. Twice the gain produces strong DLM output even without added sensory input. These bounded tests expose sensitivity and a global-gain tradeoff; they do not identify a biological parameter set. Production weights and physiology remain unchanged.

The [matched performance report](../transplant-performance-solid-wing-repair.md) contains eight valid measurements on this frozen production build. BANC is 1.65× slower in Reference and 1.68× slower in Fast than the retained FlyWire system; every paired trial passes the requested factor-of-ten threshold. BANC advances at 0.03868× and 0.04563× real time, respectively. This is product throughput with the original UI and active inspection, not equal workloads or equal rendered-frame counts. The experimental flexible-wing prototype is excluded.
