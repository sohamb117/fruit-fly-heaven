## Behavioral result

The direct BANC runs show unwanted wing activation, departures from the fruit, inversion and uncontrolled tumbling. The final 24-frame segment progressed from 0 to 3.122 simulated seconds without error: it launched, lost posture beside the banana, and ended overturned. All 24 samples remained inside the recorded habitat bound; mouth contact and ingestion stayed zero. The corrected contact-specific run has not demonstrated successful feeding or the complete requested localization → approach → landing → probing/feeding → takeoff/flight cycle. Staying inside a collision bowl is not evidence of controlled flight.

Earlier apparent feeding cannot be carried forward as evidence: the old pooled-mouth decoder routed m8/m7 activity into extension. The [observed motor-gap analysis](motor-gap.md) records the actual sampled muscle drive and missing coordination. The physical body has working upstream walking/flight controllers, but those policies have not been distilled into the BANC muscle bridge. A separate [common-body landing/walking teacher](../flybody-landing-teacher/README.md) provides native trajectories with exact state continuity; the reverse takeoff handoff is still unsuccessful.

No food-following command, desired root pose, pose clamp or external stabilizing force was added to make direct BANC behavior look successful. The remaining problem includes calibrating coordinated sensory–neural–muscle control; the existence of a validated body model does not validate the current motor output.

The [wing contract audit](../flybody-wing-contract-audit.md) found no established index, sign or timebase error in the wing path. It quantifies consequential remaining assumptions: powered target-restoring servos at zero DLM/DVM drive, a steep 85% deployment gate, and shared DLM/DVM averaging. Those assumptions were not retuned merely to make a flight look stable. The [terrain audit](../fruit-surface-audit.md) distinguishes the bowl winding from smaller polygon/heightfield differences; the subsequent endpoint repair removes false rounded supports. Heightfield resolution and its inability to represent overhangs remain explicit approximations.

A subsequent [400 ms controlled onset replay](../flybody-onset-causal/README.md) reproduces the recorded browser trajectory with zero qpos/qvel/control/muscle-state error. The normal input loses support at 104 ms and is inverted by the 112 ms sample; removing only DLM/DVM input keeps the replay upright and supported. Removing the remaining active wing servo adds almost no change. This isolates the active wing path for further mechanics/sensory calibration; it does not justify silently disabling flight or claim recovered behavior. A subsequent [native-step mechanics audit](../flybody-onset-causal/mechanics.md) places first inversion at 110.05 ms, before the first wing impact at 112.70 ms. Wing–terrain impacts amplify the later launch but do not initiate the tumble.

The [contact geometry classification](../flybody-onset-causal/contact-geometry.md) identifies some later wing contacts in invisible banana fill and at a native cliff 6.43 mm above the visible bowl. The largest sampled normal force occurs on the real bowl. The remaining work therefore has two distinct parts: accurate fruit collision geometry preserving legitimate contacts, and the wing-control instability that starts before wing impacts. Both remain unresolved in the default model.
