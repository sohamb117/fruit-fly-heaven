Model scope and evidence

The chemical graph is BANC v888 with the v3 synapse detector. Cell-type-dependent membrane parameters, receptor kinetics, graded release, adaptation and neuromodulation are mechanistic priors. Their numerical values have not been measured in this specimen or fitted to complete behavior. Glutamate defaults to a chloride receptor in the CNS; explicit receptor overrides can change it. Unknown transmitters are never assigned an invented excitatory sign.

Selected GF–PSI electrical connections are literature-based additions with assumed conductance. Muscles have activation, fatigue and force–length–velocity dynamics. The reduced FlyBody fit covers local kinematics and inertia; contact forces, muscle moment arms, averaged flight forces and coarse sensory tuning remain approximations. This is an experimental substrate, not a demonstrated autonomous feeding-and-flight cycle.
