What is real, and what is modeled?

The wiring is real.

Every one of the source model’s 15,091,983 directed connection rows is included, representing 54,492,922 synapses. The flies share this immutable wiring; their voltages, synaptic currents, spike histories, and random inputs are independent.

The downloaded v783 computational model contains 138,639 neurons, compared with 139,255 in FlyWire’s classification. No additional neurons or connections were removed here.

The electrical activity is a model.

Reference mode uses Float64 state, a 0.1 ms threshold grid, 20 ms membrane decay, 5 ms synaptic decay, 1.8 ms transmission delay, and a 2.2 ms refractory period. Fast mode uses Float32 state, a 1 ms grid, and 2 ms delay/refractory periods. This approximation can change spike timing and activity. Neurotransmitter signs are predictions supplied by the source model.

The event-based engine analytically updates quiet cells when needed. Every neural step is accounted for; rendering does not advance the neural clock.

The habitat is supplied.

Fruit odor stimulates measured DM1/VA2 olfactory cells; contact stimulates sugar/water sensory cells. Concentration and firing-rate mappings are assumptions. Forward speed and turning are decoded from descending-cell activity; the legs use a supplied animation. The ventral nerve cord and flight physics are absent.

Food is inexhaustible. Metabolism, reproduction, learning, and subjective experience are not modeled. This is not a validated digital replica of a living fly.
